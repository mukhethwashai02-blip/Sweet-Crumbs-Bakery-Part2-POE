Frequently Asked Questions

Can I place a custom cake order?

Yes. You can use our Order Now page to enquire about a custom cake.

Where can I collect my order?

You can request collection in Rosebank or Sandton.

How much do your products cost?

Our chocolate cake costs R180, fresh bread costs R25 and pastries cost R20. Custom order prices must be confirmed.

Is my order confirmed when I submit the form?

No. The form provides an estimated price. The bakery must confirm availability and your order.
